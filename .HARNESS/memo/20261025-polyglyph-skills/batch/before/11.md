<subcommands>
economics.exe: wrapper CLI -> delegates to sub-binaries in same dir.

1. estat [japan_stats]:
   q: `economics estat --stats-data <ID>` ∨ `economics estat --search <WORD>`
   env: ESTAT_API_KEY

2. edinet [japan_corporate_filings]:
   q: `economics edinet --date YYYY-MM-DD` ∨ `economics edinet --doc-id <ID>`
   env: EDINET_SUBSCRIPTION_KEY

3. sec [us_sec_edgar_filings]:
   q: `economics sec --ticker <SYMBOL> [--facts|--submissions]`
   env: N/A (Keyless, auto-UA)

4. fred [us_global_macro_series]:
   q: `economics fred --series <SERIES_ID>` (e.g. FEDFUNDS, CPIAUCSL, GDP, DEXJPUS)
   env: FRED_API_KEY

5. worldbank [global_indicators]:
   q: `economics worldbank --country <CODE> --indicator <CODE>` (e.g. JPN, NY.GDP.MKTP.CD)
   env: N/A (Keyless)

6. yfinance [market_data_chart]:
   q: `economics yfinance --symbol <TICKER> [--range 1mo] [--summary]`
   env: N/A (Keyless)

7. alphavantage [stock_fx_data]:
   q: `economics alphavantage --symbol <TICKER>` ∨ `--function CURRENCY_EXCHANGE_RATE --from USD --to JPY`
   env: ALPHAVANTAGE_API_KEY
</subcommands>
